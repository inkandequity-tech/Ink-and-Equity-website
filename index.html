The Firm · 01

A legacy
reforged.

01 Founded 2017. Reforged as Ink & Equity in 2024.
02 Practice across UP & Delhi NCR with appearances before the Supreme Court of India, High Courts, and tribunals.
03 Counsel to private clients, corporates, and government bodies — from individual matters to institutional mandates.

The practice began in 2017 as Rishabh Raj & Associates — a chamber built on long hours, careful drafting, and a refusal to cut corners. Over seven years, it served clients across Uttar Pradesh and Delhi NCR, earning trust through plain dealing and the patience to see matters through.

"Ink" for the discipline of the written record. "Equity" for the principle that holds the work together.

In 2024, the chamber became Ink & Equity LLP — a deliberate widening of scope, structure, and ambition. Same hands. Sharper instrument. The name itself is an argument: that the craft of law lives in the meticulous draftsmanship of documents and the constant pursuit of fair, just outcomes for the people who place their matters in our care.

Today, the firm advises on real estate and RERA matters, constitutional and service-law disputes, taxation, civil and sessions court litigation, criminal defence, and corporate advisory. Among our institutional clients have been Knoxx (Capital Novus), the Sultanpur Zila Panchayat, the Ghaziabad Development Authority, and Deen Dayal Upadhyaya University, Meerut, among other public bodies and private enterprises.

We measure ourselves not by volume but by outcome — by whether the client's position is meaningfully better after they leave the chamber than it was when they walked in.

When the wrong person files: Power of Attorney, Section 39, and the limits of regulatory correction in real estate disputes.

The Court found that the original complaint, filed by a Power of Attorney holder rather than the allottee himself, was not valid — and that an order under Section 39 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, attempting to substitute the complainant's name, exceeded the section's scope. We acted for the Respondent. A short analysis of why this matters for investors and developers alike, with practical takeaways for documentation.
